Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026.

The short version

Perch doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cloud service.

What Perch does on your Mac

  • It shows files from your Downloads folder, and files you drop on it, in its trays. It keeps a list of those files, and copies it makes when you rename or convert something, in ~/Library/Application Support/Perch on your Mac.
  • It takes screenshots only when you press your screenshot keys. They stay on your Mac.
  • It keeps a small activity log in ~/Library/Logs/Perch.log to help diagnose problems. It records events such as "screenshot taken", sometimes with a file's name, but never what's inside your files, and it never leaves your Mac unless you choose to send it to us.
  • If you convert a presentation, it asks Keynote on your Mac to export it. Nothing is uploaded.

The network

Perch itself doesn't connect to the internet. When you choose AirDrop or Share, macOS's own sharing handles the file, the same as from any other app. Menu items such as Perch Website and Send Feedback simply open a page in your browser.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and downloads are served by GitHub. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. The site itself uses no cookies or analytics.
